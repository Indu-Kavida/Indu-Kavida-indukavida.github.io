Power BI

Quickbite Express Crisis Recovery Analysis | Power BI

✧ Built an end-to-end Power BI dashboard for a food-delivery platform analyzing 108K customers and 149K orders, tracking retention (12.99%) and average spend (₹461.7).

✧ Identified crisis impact with a 68.9% order decline, drop in monthly active users from 22K → 8K, and higher cancellations (7.45%), enabling data-driven insights by city and time.

✧ Analyzed delivery performance across cities showing 95.2K delayed deliveries, 44.4 min avg delivery time, and low 36.16% SLA compliance, highlighting operational bottlenecks.

UPI Spending Patterns Analysis | Power BI

✧ Analyzed personal UPI spending patterns using Power BI, tracking a ₹24.05K monthly average spend across 13.8K bill payments and 5.7K exam-fee transactions.

✧ Identified monthly expense spikes, with highest spends in March (₹62.3K) and December (₹62.5K) through income vs expense trend analysis.

✧ Broke down category-wise spending, showing Bills (₹13.8K) as the largest share, followed by Exam Fees (₹5.74K), Groceries (₹0.98K), and Food (₹0.96K).
